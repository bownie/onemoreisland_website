Last updated: 19 November 2026.

This policy explains what happens when you use **onemoreislandbook.com**, and in particular when you sign up to the newsletter.

## Who is responsible for your data

The site and newsletter are run by Richard Bown, who is the data controller for the information described here. You can reach him through [richardwbown.com](https://richardwbown.com) or by replying to any newsletter email.

## What I collect and why

**If you sign up to the newsletter**, I collect the email address you submit (and your name, if you choose to give it). I use it for one purpose only: to send you occasional updates about _One More Island_, Richard Bown's writing, publication news and early reader offers.

The legal basis for this is your **consent**, which you give by ticking the consent box and confirming your subscription. Every signup requires a confirmation email before you are added to the list, so nothing is sent to you until you have opted in twice. When you tick the box I also record the date and time, and the wording you agreed to, against your subscription so there is a record of your consent.

**If you simply browse the site**, I collect standard analytics data as described below. I do not run advertising, tracking pixels or profiling.

## Emails and unsubscribing

Every newsletter email contains a one-click unsubscribe link. You can also ask me to remove you by replying to any email. When you unsubscribe, your address is removed from the active list and kept only on a suppression list so you are not emailed again by accident.

I do not sell, rent or trade your email address, and I do not share it with anyone except the email service that delivers the newsletter.

## Who processes the data

The newsletter is delivered using **MailerLite**, which stores subscriber addresses and sends the emails on my behalf. MailerLite acts as a data processor under a data processing agreement and may process data outside the UK/EEA using standard contractual safeguards. You can read their privacy policy at [mailerlite.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://www.mailerlite.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## Analytics and cookies

The site uses **Google Analytics 4** to count visits and see which pages are read. This sets cookies (typically `_ga` and `_ga_*`) that record an approximate location, the pages you view, the site you arrived from and your device type. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used.

You can block or delete these cookies in your browser at any time, and the site will keep working normally. To opt out of Google Analytics across all sites, install the [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

The site also stores a small preference in your browser's local storage to remember whether you chose the light or dark theme. That stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

## How long I keep it

Your email address stays on the newsletter list until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is retained for the period configured in Google Analytics (currently up to 14 months) and then deleted automatically.

## Your rights

You can ask me at any time to:

- tell you what data I hold about you;
- correct anything that is wrong;
- delete your data;
- restrict or object to how it is used;
- withdraw your consent, which stops any future newsletter emails.

Ask by replying to any newsletter email or through [richardwbown.com](https://richardwbown.com). I will respond within one month. If you are not happy with how I have handled your request, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change too, and any significant change affecting newsletter subscribers will be mentioned in an email.
